Privacy Policy — Depths of Shadow Reflections

Effective: August 23, 2026

Depths of Shadow Reflections is designed as a local-first self-reflection application. In the current version, journal entries, feeling check-ins, journey progress, and pattern maps are stored locally on the user's device. The app does not include advertising, analytics, user accounts, or third-party tracking SDKs and does not intentionally transmit journal content to the developer.

Data controls

Users may export their locally stored data or erase it using the controls inside the app. Clearing browser or application storage may also erase local data.

Changes

This policy will be updated before any release that adds cloud synchronization, accounts, analytics, AI services, payments, advertising, or other data collection.

Contact

Before Google Play submission, replace this paragraph with the developer's public privacy contact email or contact mechanism.
